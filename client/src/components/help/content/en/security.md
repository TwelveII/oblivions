# Password & email

Open **Settings → Security**.

## Password

Change your password any time — enter your current one and a new one.

![the change-password form](assets/security-1.png)

## Email (optional)

Add an **email** to enable **password recovery**, or change/remove it later. Email changes ask for your password to confirm it's really you.

![the email section in Security settings](assets/security-2.png)

## End-to-end encryption

MQVI requires end-to-end encryption for new direct messages, channel messages, and voice-channel chat. It cannot be switched off.

Set up encryption on each device and save a recovery password in **Settings → Encryption**. Without the device keys or a recovery backup, that device cannot decrypt your encrypted message history.

Messages sent before mandatory encryption was introduced remain legacy plaintext history; they are not retroactively encrypted.
